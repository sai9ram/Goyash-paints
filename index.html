Application Guide

SURFACE PREPARATION & APPLICATION GUIDE

PERFECT PREPARATION. PERFECT FINISH. The right preparation and application ensures long lasting beauty and performance of Goyash Paints.

1

1. CLEAN SURFACE

Ensure the surface is clean, dry and free from dust, dirt, grease, loose paint, algae or any contaminants.

2

2. REPAIR SURFACE

Fill cracks and holes with suitable putty and allow it to dry completely.

3

3. SANDING

Sand the surface smooth using emery paper and wipe clean.

4

4. PRIMER APPLICATION

Apply one coat of Goyash Primer or Goyash Primer White and allow it to dry.

5

5. TOP COAT APPLICATION

Apply 2 coats of Goyash Emulsion for a beautiful & durable finish.

6

6. PERFECT FINISH

Allow proper drying between coats for best results and long lasting beauty.

RECOMMENDED TOOLS

  • Brush: Professional synthetic bristle paint brush.
  • Roller: Smooth & medium pile paint roller.
  • Spray Gun: Airless spray application system.
  • Putty Knife: Stainless steel wall prep spatula.
  • Sand Paper: 180 & 320 grit emery paper.
  • Tray: Heavy duty paint mixing roller tray.

*Use good quality tools for better finish and higher coverage.

APPLICATION TIPS

  • ✓ Apply in shade and on cool surface.
  • ✓ Do not apply when surface temperature is below 10°C or above 40°C.
  • ✓ Stir well before use.
  • ✓ Use clean water for thinning.
  • ✓ Apply second coat after recommended drying time (4-6 hours).
  • ✓ Close the container tightly after use.
  • ✓ Clean tools with water immediately after use.

PRO TIPS & SAFETY

  • ✓ Avoid exterior painting during rain or high humidity.
  • ✓ Ensure surface is completely dry before applying.
  • ✓ Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool dry place.
  • ✓ In case of contact with eyes, wash immediately with plenty of water.
